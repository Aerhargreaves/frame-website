BESPOKE


Working with bespoke clients is one of my favourite ways to bring a new frame to life.
The process is a true collaboration.
Each frame is woven with personal details, handmade and tailored to you.
A true one-off.

PROCESS

Two to three months

1. Consultation
This is an opportunity to share ideas and ambitions for the project ahead.
Here we can discuss any specific fit issues or details you would like to focus on.

2. Design
This stage involves two date, including our first in person meeting.
Throughout a 4 week period we will take your facial measurements, present ideas and select a chosen shape and material to move forward with.

3. Prototype
The technical phase of the project. This is all about achieving the perfect fit and ensuring any custom components are included in the design.

4. Delivery
After a 4-5 week making period, you will get to see your frames for the first time.
During this meeting I will make final adjustments to ensure you take home the perfect frame.

Starting at £1,000


Your frame will always be fitted with traditional pinned hinges, that way it can be repaired for years to come.
To reflect this, I offer free repairs for 2 years after your frames are complete. The aim, to create a future heirloom.
