MONC REPAIR CART

Reintergrating craft into the repairs conversation through the visual narrative of a traditional ‘street vendor’.

While Innovation has become synonymous with sustainability, repair is considered a low-cost necessity. By bringing the workshop outside we are able to facilitate conversations that connect people to their objects and build respect for those that can fix them.

The Repair Cart forms part of an inhouse research project into the economic viability of repair services in Fashion & Accessories. A familiar and friendly visual language is used to facilitate 1-2-1 interviews with members of the public about repairs and object sentimentality.

The insights gained were used to inform MONC’s future repairs program and greater approach to circularity.


Construction & Materiality:

The main structure is made up of two modules that detach for ease of storage. Intergrating hemp tiles, recycled counter top and aluminium offcuts allowed us to mimic MONC’s store identity and create a cohesive material identity. The cart’s tools are entirely powered by an inbuilt solar system.

*This was a collaborative project, working alongside Bath-based maker & artist Jake Kaliszewski
